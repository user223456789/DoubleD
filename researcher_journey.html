Resolved open
questions

The five yellow-highlighted items from the end-to-end process document, and the call made on each to keep things moving.

Q1NDA timing — before info shared?
"NDA auto executed at this point before information provided for review?"
Resolved — Yes. The NDA is auto-generated and e-signed by both parties the moment the researcher accepts, before any company materials are shared. For consultations the same applies: accept → NDA executes → then scheduling and materials.
Q2Clarifying questions — how?
"Requests clarifying questions? (do so via email correspondence?)"
Resolved — In-platform, not email. Built into the scheduling flow (step 3 of the call setup). Keeps all correspondence under the NDA and auditable, rather than scattered across email. The investor is notified and responds through the platform.
Q3Who hosts the video call?
"Platform used to host video call? Or individual meeting link provided by researcher?"
Resolved — Researcher provides the link. For MVP, the researcher pastes their own Zoom/Teams/Meet link during scheduling. The platform stores it and includes it in the calendar invite. Avoids building video infrastructure now; can revisit a native solution later.
Q4Profile form reference
"(see other researcher profile doc)"
Resolved — Fields aligned to the template. The full profile (in the registration flow) now reflects the profile template: one-liner, primary/secondary expertise, short + full bio, industry/advisory experience, Scopus ID, ORCID, career summary, availability per fortnight, and the compliance checkboxes including IP acknowledgement and liability insurance.
Q5Welcome & onboarding materials
"welcome email and onboarding materials (to be created)"
Resolved — Welcome page built. The post-approval Welcome & Onboarding page includes an activation checklist (Stripe setup, university approval) and four onboarding guides: how reviews work, writing a strong report, getting paid, and conflicts & compliance.
