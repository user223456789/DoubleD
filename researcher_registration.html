Step 1 of 5 — Identity Verification

Verify your
academic identity

Deep Diligence is exclusively for active researchers at Australian universities and public research institutions. We verify your identity before approving your profile.

How we verify your identity
All applications are manually reviewed before approval. We cross-check three things:
  • Your institutional email domain matches a recognised Australian university or research body
  • Your staff or researcher profile URL is publicly listed on your institution's website
  • Your ORCID iD (if provided) matches your stated publication record
01 Institutional Email Address

Must be your current institutional email address. Personal Gmail, Hotmail or similar addresses will not be accepted.

Recognised institution domains:

unsw.edu.au unimelb.edu.au sydney.edu.au anu.edu.au uq.edu.au monash.edu uwa.edu.au csiro.au ansto.gov.au curtin.edu.au adelaide.edu.au + 38 more
02 Institutional Profile Page

Link to your public profile on your institution's website — the page that lists your role, research interests, and publications. This is our primary identity verification check.

03 ORCID iD Strongly recommended
ORCID
What is ORCID? ORCID is a free, globally recognised researcher identifier used by universities, journals, and funding bodies worldwide. It provides a verified, persistent link between you and your publications. If you don't have one, you can register free at orcid.org — it takes 2 minutes. Providing an ORCID iD speeds up your application review significantly.

University Approval for Paid External Work
Most Australian universities require staff to obtain prior approval before undertaking paid outside work — including consulting engagements. This applies to Deep Diligence reviews. Requirements vary by institution: some require a simple notification, others a formal conflict-of-interest declaration or approval from your Head of School.

You are responsible for obtaining the necessary approval from your institution before accepting any paid engagements through Deep Diligence. We recommend doing this before completing your registration. We have prepared a template letter you can use as a starting point.

Manual Review Queue
What happens next
Every application is reviewed by a member of the Deep Diligence team before approval. This is how we maintain the quality and integrity of the researcher network.
01 Profile verification — We check your institutional email domain, cross-reference your staff profile URL, and verify your stated affiliation. ORCID iD matched to publication record if provided.
02 Domain expertise review — We assess whether your stated expertise matches your publication record and institutional profile. We may reach out for clarification.
03 Approval email — If approved, you'll receive an email with a link to activate your researcher account, set your password, and complete Stripe identity verification for payments.
04 First review request — Once active, you'll receive notification when a firm's request matches your domain. You'll have 24 hours to accept or decline. No pressure, no commitment to volume.
NOTE 01
Get your university approval in the meantime
If you haven't already obtained approval from your institution for paid outside work, now is a good time to do so — before your first review request arrives. Your application won't be delayed by this, but you'll need approval before accepting any paid engagement.
